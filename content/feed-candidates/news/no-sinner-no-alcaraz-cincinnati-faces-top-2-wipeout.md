World No. 1 Jannik Sinner visited Milan's Physioclinic orthopaedic clinic on Tuesday for what Corriere della Sera reports was a knee check-up — not a routine appointment — with a second examination not yet ruled out before Cincinnati begins August 13. His team has not confirmed the report, but skipping the tournament is reportedly on the table. That would compound an already seismic blow to the draw: No. 2 Carlos Alcaraz officially withdrew on August 4 with a lingering right wrist injury that has kept him out since April, putting his US Open title defence in serious doubt. With both top seeds potentially absent from the last Masters 1000 before Flushing Meadows, the Cincinnati draw cracks wide open — and whoever lifts the trophy arrives at the US Open with a massive momentum edge over a wounded top-2.
