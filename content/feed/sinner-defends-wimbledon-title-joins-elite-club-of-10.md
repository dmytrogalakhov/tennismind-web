Jannik Sinner defeated Alexander Zverev in Sunday's Wimbledon final in a match The Guardian described as 'short of subtlety but oozing muscular power,' with boom-boom tennis returning to Centre Court. The result sees Sinner retain the title he won last year, completing back-to-back Wimbledon championships. With the Mubadala DC Open hard-court swing beginning in 10 days, Sinner arrives as the undisputed world No. 1 and a two-time Wimbledon champion — a weight of expectation that will follow him all the way to the US Open.
