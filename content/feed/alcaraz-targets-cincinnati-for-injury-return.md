Carlos Alcaraz has entered the Cincinnati Open as he maps out his return from a wrist injury that has kept him sidelined. The Mubadala DC Open begins in nine days, and Cincinnati follows shortly after — Alcaraz's entry signals he is targeting the hard-court swing rather than rushing back earlier. With Alcaraz absent from the early hard-court events, the top of draws in Washington and beyond opens up for players like Sinner, Zverev, and Fritz to accumulate ranking points unchallenged.
