Felix Auger-Aliassime has withdrawn from the National Bank Open in Montreal due to injury, ruling him out of the event he called his home tournament. The Canadian, who arrived in Montreal last week for a pre-tournament media availability, will not take to the court in front of his home fans. The withdrawal means Canada's men's challenge at the NBO falls to others, with Auger-Aliassime missing a Masters 1000 on hard court just weeks before the US Open.
