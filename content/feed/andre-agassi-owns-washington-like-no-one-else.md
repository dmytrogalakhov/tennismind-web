Andre Agassi won the Washington title five times — more than any other player in the tournament's 57-year history, which stretches back to 1969. The next closest record in doubles belongs to three players tied at four titles each: Marty Riessen, Mike Bryan, and Bob Bryan. That Agassi dominance on hard courts in Washington is a reminder that the DC Open has historically rewarded players who make hard courts their identity.
