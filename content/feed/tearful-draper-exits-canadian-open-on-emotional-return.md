Draper, returning from a lengthy injury absence, was visibly emotional mid-match during his first-round defeat at the Canadian Open, breaking down in tears on court. The loss means his comeback begins with an R1 exit at a Masters 1000 hard-court event. With the US Open roughly three weeks away, Draper now has limited opportunities on the North American swing to rebuild match sharpness before the final Slam of the season.
