The Mubadala DC Open is the only combined ATP & WTA 500-level tournament in the world — a status it has held since the 2023 merger of the Citi Open and the Mubadala Silicon Valley Classic. Founded in 1969, it is also the fourth-longest running pro tennis event in the United States and the longest-running tournament held in an equal-access public park. That dual 500-level status means both men's and women's draws carry identical ranking points and prize money prestige, making Washington structurally unique on the entire global tennis calendar.
