First staged in 1881 at the Toronto Lawn Tennis Club — four years after the first Wimbledon — the National Bank Open predates the US Open, making it the third-oldest tennis tournament in the world. The 2026 edition marks the 145th anniversary of the men's ATP event, a span no non-Grand Slam tournament comes close to matching. That longevity explains why the NBO carries genuine prestige beyond its Masters 1000 points: it is the oldest title a player can win outside of a Slam.
