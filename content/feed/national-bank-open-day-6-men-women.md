MEN'S DRAW
Montreal said goodbye to one of the game's great entertainers — and a young American made sure the farewell was earned.

• Learner Tien 🇺🇸 def. Gael Monfils 🇫🇷, 6-3, 0-6, 6-3 — Monfils confirmed this is his final professional tournament, and he went out fighting: a stunning 6-0 second set silenced the crowd before Tien steadied himself to close it out. A standing ovation followed. The torch passes.
• Tommy Paul 🇺🇸 def. Valentin Royer 🇫🇷, 6-4, 7-6 — Paul grinds through a tight second set to stay on course in Montreal, keeping American hopes alive deep in the draw.
• Titouan Droguet 🇫🇷 def. Jaime Faria 🇵🇹, 7-6, 6-2 — the unseeded French qualifier converts a nervy first set into a commanding win, proving his run is no fluke.

WOMEN'S DRAW
Toronto's draw is shaping up around a champion in form and a crowd desperate for a home hero.

• Alexandra Eala 🇵🇭 def. Alycia Parks 🇺🇸, 6-1, 4-6, 6-2 — one week after claiming her first WTA title in Washington, Eala avenges an Australian Open loss to Parks and arrives in Toronto as a genuine threat.
• Leylah Fernandez 🇨🇦 advanced — the hometown favourite is through on Centre Court, and the crowd in Toronto is ready to believe.
