Botic van de Zandschulp knocked out Daniil Medvedev and Argentina's Thiago Tirante eliminated Taylor Fritz on the same day in Montreal, sending two of the tournament's top seeds packing in the second round. The double upset clears a significant portion of the top half of the draw at a Masters 1000 event on hard court. With the US Open weeks away, both Medvedev and Fritz now head into the final Slam of the season without the ranking points a deep Montreal run would have provided.
