Alexandra Eala advanced to the third round of the National Bank Open in Toronto with a 6-1, 4-6, 6-2 win over Alycia Parks, avenging her three-set first-round loss to the American at this year's Australian Open. The match was played in front of a sold-out Centre Court crowd — tickets had sold out almost the moment Eala's match time was announced, following a Washington final that sold out within an hour of her reaching it. The 21-year-old is now riding a wave of momentum into the US Open hard-court swing having won her first WTA 500 title last week in Washington.
