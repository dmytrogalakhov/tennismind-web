MEN'S DRAW
Day 5 in Montreal was a demolition derby for the top of the draw — the defending champion is gone, and so is nearly every other marquee name.

• Tallon Griekspoor 🇳🇱 def. Alexander Zverev 🇩🇪 — the world No. 1 and defending champion exits in straight sets, blowing the draw wide open for everyone still standing.
• Botic van de Zandschulp 🇳🇱 def. Daniil Medvedev 🇷🇺 — two Dutch players eliminating two of the top four seeds on the same day is not a coincidence; it's a statement about hard-court parity in 2025.
• Thiago Tirante 🇦🇷 def. Taylor Fritz 🇺🇸 — the world No. 7 never found his footing, and Tirante's aggressive baseline game punished every hesitation.
• Joao Fonseca 🇧🇷 def. Stefanos Tsitsipas 🇬🇷 — the 18-year-old keeps collecting scalps; this is no longer a surprise, which is itself the story.

WOMEN'S DRAW
Toronto's third round delivered a mix of expected results and one sharp upset that reshuffles the upper half.

• Catherine McNally 🇺🇸 def. Linda Noskova 🇨🇿 — the Wimbledon champion and sixth seed is out, giving McNally her biggest win of the season and clearing a path for the American contingent.
• Alina Korneeva 🇷🇺 def. Emma Navarro 🇺🇸 — a 6-2, 6-2 dismantling of a top-ten player signals Korneeva is playing well above her ranking this week.
• Elena Rybakina 🇰🇿 def. Daria Kasatkina 🇦🇺 — a three-set grind shows Rybakina is not yet at her most clinical, but she's through and that's what matters.
• Mirra Andreeva 🇷🇺 def. Karolina Pliskova 🇨🇿 — a 6-0, 6-2 scoreline from a 17-year-old against a former world No. 1 needs no further explanation.
